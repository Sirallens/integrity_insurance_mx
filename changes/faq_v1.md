# Mexico Tourist Auto Insurance – Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is Mexico Tourist Auto Insurance?
Mexico Tourist Auto Insurance is a temporary auto insurance policy that provides liability and physical damage coverage for vehicles driven from the United States or Canada into Mexico.

### Do I need Mexican insurance if I already have U.S. auto insurance?
Yes. U.S. and Canadian auto insurance policies are not legally recognized in Mexico. Mexican law requires liability coverage issued by a Mexican insurance company.

### Is Mexico Tourist Insurance required by law?
Yes. Mexican federal law requires drivers to carry liability insurance from a Mexican insurer when driving on federal highways and roads.

### How long can a Mexico tourist auto insurance policy last?
Policies can typically be purchased for:
- 1 day
- 3 days
- 7 days
- 15 days
- 30 days
- 6 months
- 1 year

### Who can buy a Mexico Tourist Insurance policy?
Anyone driving a U.S. or Canadian plated vehicle into Mexico can purchase a policy.

## Coverage Questions

### What does Mexico Tourist Auto Insurance cover?
Coverage may include:
- Liability (required by law)
- Physical damage (collision & theft)
- Medical payments
- Legal assistance
- Roadside assistance
- Bail bond coverage

### What is liability coverage in Mexico?
Liability insurance covers damage or injury you cause to third parties. This coverage is mandatory in Mexico.

### Does the policy cover theft of my vehicle?
Yes, if you purchase full coverage which includes theft protection.

### Does the policy cover medical expenses?
Most policies include medical payments coverage for the driver and passengers.

### Does the policy include legal assistance?
Yes. Many policies include legal representation and bail bond coverage, which is very important in case of an accident in Mexico.

## Vehicle & Driver Questions

### What vehicles can be insured?
Most tourist policies cover:
- Cars
- SUVs
- Pickup trucks
- Motorhomes

Certain vehicles such as commercial trucks or exotic vehicles may require special policies.

### Can multiple drivers be covered?
Yes. Most policies allow additional drivers as long as they have a valid driver’s license.

## Travel Questions

### Do I need insurance if I only drive near the border?
Yes. Even in border zones, Mexican law requires liability insurance issued by a Mexican company.

### Does the insurance work anywhere in Mexico?
Yes. Tourist auto policies cover you throughout Mexico, depending on the coverage you choose.

### What happens if I have an accident in Mexico?
Call the insurance company’s claims number immediately. An adjuster will be sent to the accident location to assist you.

### What documents should I carry when driving in Mexico?
You should always carry:
- Your insurance policy
- Valid driver’s license
- Vehicle registration
- Passport or travel documents
- Temporary Vehicle Import Permit for Mexico

## Claims & Assistance

### What should I do if my car breaks down in Mexico?
Most policies include roadside assistance, which may help with towing, tire changes, or mechanical problems.

### Do I have to pay anything if I have a claim?
If you have physical damage coverage, you may have a deductible depending on the policy.

### Is assistance available in English?
Yes. Most tourist auto insurance providers offer 24/7 bilingual assistance.
